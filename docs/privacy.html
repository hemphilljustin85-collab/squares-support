Squares Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Squares is a game-day board organization and score-tracking app. This policy explains the information Squares processes and why.

Information processed

  • Account information: If you create a cloud account, Squares processes your email address and an account identifier for authentication and account management.
  • User content: Squares processes board names, team names, display names, selected squares, join codes, scores, and quarter results so users can create, join, share, and manage boards.
  • Local demo data: Signed-out demo boards are stored on the device.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide authentication, synchronize cloud boards, operate requested app features, maintain security, and respond to support requests.

Service provider

Squares uses Supabase for cloud authentication, database storage, and synchronization. Supabase processes information on our behalf to provide these functions.

No tracking, advertising, or sale

Squares does not use data to track users across other companies' apps or websites, does not display third-party advertising, and does not sell personal information.

No payment handling

Squares does not collect, transfer, hold, or distribute participant money, payments, wagers, entry fees, or prizes. Any informational square value refers only to arrangements made independently and outside the app.

Retention and deletion

Cloud account and board information is retained while needed to provide the service. Signed-in users can permanently delete their account and associated cloud data directly in the app by opening Your account and selecting Delete account, then confirming the deletion. Local demo data can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its storage.

Content moderation

Board names, team names, and display names are visible to other members of the same board. Reports you send from the app open an email to us containing the board code and the reported name so we can review it.

Children

Squares is not directed to children under 13.
